        AI key

        
        

          
The check-in talks to Google's Gemini API straight from this phone. There
            is no server in between and no account — your key and your messages go directly from here
            to Google.

          
Sent: what you type, and — while "Answer using my data" is on — a
            summary of your logged numbers: training, sleep, weight, eating.
            Never sent: camera frames, pose data, or face images. Those are read on this device
            and thrown away frame by frame; nothing in this app has ever kept an image.

          
Which key you paste changes what Google may do with it. Google's
            terms say that on the free tier, submitted content is used to improve their products and
            human reviewers may read it; on a billing-enabled key, that it is not used to improve
            their products. The app behaves identically either way — the difference is the key.
            Gemini API terms ·
            your keys

          
The key is stored on this device in plain text — anyone holding the
            unlocked phone can read it — so set a spend limit on it. Everything else in this app
            works without one.
